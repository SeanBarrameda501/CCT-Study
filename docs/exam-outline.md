### Conflict between CCI documents
S3 (June 2022 PDF) lists five domains: Pre-Procedural 10%, Performing ECG 39%, Stress 10%, Ambulatory 4%,
Rhythm Analysis 37%. S1 (current web page) splits "Performing ECG" into **Resting ECG 14%** and
**Analyzing 12-Lead ECG 22%** and raises **Rhythm Analysis to 40%**. We follow **S1** because it is the
current official page. Task wording is otherwise nearly identical.

## Exam format (S1, S2)

- Computer-based, Pearson VUE professional centers, year-round.
- **130 questions: 110 scored + 20 unscored pretest items** (unidentified, randomly placed).
- **2 hours total: 1 h 50 min for questions + 10 min tutorial and post-exam survey.**
- Scaled score 0–900; **650 required to pass**. CCI states scaled scores are *not* the same as percent correct,
  so the app's pass estimate uses a percent-correct proxy (see "App assumptions").
- Unofficial score report at the test center shows total score, pass/fail and percent correct per content area.
- No penalty for skipping, changing or revisiting questions (S2).
- Fee $175 USD (S1/S3).

### Test-day ID rules (S1, re-read 2026-11-23)
- Bring **two original, unexpired IDs** (no photocopies). The first and last name must **exactly match** the name you registered with.
- **Primary ID:** government-issued, with name, recent photo and signature (passport, driver's license, military ID, state/national ID card, alien registration card).
- **Secondary ID:** any ID with name and signature, or name and a recent recognizable photo.
- To reschedule, contact Pearson VUE **at least two full working days** in advance. One 90-day extension of the Authorization to Test may be requested.
- Results: an unofficial score report at the test center; the official letter and certificate follow about 15 business days later.

### Item types (S1 "Question Types", S2)
1. Traditional multiple choice – one of four options (A–D) correct.
2. Multiple response – two or three correct from four to six options ("Select two correct responses").
3. Hot spot – click one area of an image.
4. Drag and place – drag labels onto areas of an image (not all tokens used).

S1 also contains an older sentence saying "each question is shown with four possible answers, only one of which is
the correct or best answer". That conflicts with the multiple-response and drag-and-place descriptions on the same page.
The app mirrors the full list: four-option multiple choice, select-all-that-apply, ordering (as a stand-in for
drag-and-place sequencing) and hot spot (tap a region on an ECG image).

### Task list
**A. Conducting Pre-Procedural Activities (10%)**
1. Verify physician's orders (protocol, patient limitations)
2. Verify patient identifiers (name, DOB, HIPAA)
3. Obtain patient consent (implied, informed, healthcare proxy)
4. Apply universal and/or isolation precautions
5. Perform patient transfer and transport (body mechanics, ergonomics, equipment)
6. Identify proper anatomical landmarks
7. Prepare the patient (skin prep, positioning)
8. Confirm equipment calibration, maintenance and cleaning
9. Identify safety hazards/considerations (patient, environment)
10. Provide patient education (purpose, procedure, expectations, instructions)

**B. Performing Resting ECG (14%)**
1. Gather ECG supplies and equipment
2. Troubleshoot while performing ECG (check patient, improper electrode attachment, electromagnetic interference)
3. Perform standard 12-lead ECG
4. Perform modified ECG (dextrocardia, posterior, right-sided, pediatric)

**C. Analyzing 12-Lead ECG (22%)**
1. Recognize axis deviations
2. Recognize bundle branch blocks (RBBB/LBBB)
3. Identify hypertrophy
4. Correlate ECG morphology with anatomy and physiology
5. Identify ischemic changes/infarction

**D. Performing Stress Tests (10%)**
1. Gather stress test supplies and equipment
2. Demonstrate knowledge of stress test equipment (treadmill, bicycle ergometry)
3. Perform stress test protocol (Bruce, Modified Bruce, Naughton)
4. Demonstrate knowledge of pharmacological and/or nuclear stress test protocols

**E. Performing Ambulatory Monitoring (4%)**
1. Gather ambulatory monitoring supplies and equipment
2. Verify equipment functionality pre and post patient application
3. Provide patient education on ambulatory monitoring
4. Perform ambulatory monitoring equipment and procedure troubleshooting

**F. Performing Rhythm Analysis (40%)**
1. Analyze sinus rhythm data
2. Analyze atrial rhythm data
3. Analyze junctional rhythm data
4. Analyze heart block rhythm data
5. Analyze ventricular rhythm data
6. Analyze pacemaker rhythm data
7. Report/communicate obtained rhythm data
8. Correlate ECG findings (waveforms, segments, intervals)
9. Identify life-threatening arrhythmias
10. Identify types of artifact
11. Identify classes/effects of general cardiovascular medications (beta blockers, ACE inhibitors, antiarrhythmics, etc.)

### Knowledge list (applies across tasks)
Heart size, location, layers, chambers, valves; blood flow; arteries, veins, capillaries, arterioles, venules;
cardiac valve function; pressures; cardiac output vs heart rate and stroke volume; control mechanisms;
cardiac cycle; normal values; waveforms; ECG measurement; bipolar, unipolar and precordial leads;
Einthoven's triangle and law; ECG calibration methods; single- and three-channel ECG; troubleshooting ECGs;
standardization; paper speed; lead placement; electrical interference; somatic tremor.

## App assumptions (our decisions, not CCI's)
- **Anatomy, physiology and blood flow (extra weight, by request).** A recent test-taker reported that cardiac
  anatomy and blood flow were heavily tested. CCI does not list A&P as its own domain; it appears in the knowledge list
  and in task C4. The core bank keeps the CCI percentages above. On top of it, the app adds an **anatomy bonus set**
  (tag `anatomy-bonus`, filed under domain C) covering chambers, valves, great vessels, coronary circulation, the conduction
  system and the full path of blood. These items are excluded from the proportion check and reported separately
  in the README and the validator output.
- **Patient care and communication.** These skills sit in domain A (tasks A2, A3, A5, A7, A9, A10). The app's
  patient-care section draws on domain A items plus stress and ambulatory patient-care items, so it does not change the weights.
- **Item distribution** in `data/questions.json` follows the S1 percentages (±1 percentage point).
- **Medications:** because CCI gives no sub-weight, we treat F11 as an equal share of domain F:
  40% ÷ 11 tasks ≈ **3.6% of the total**. Medication questions and flashcards are capped at that share.
- **Pass estimate:** CCI's 650/900 scaled cut is not a published percent. The app uses **≈ 70% correct**
  on a full practice exam as a rough "likely pass" proxy and labels it as an estimate.
- **Practice exam:** 130 items in 120 minutes (we keep the full two hours on the clock; the real exam gives
  1 h 50 min for questions, which the app shows as a warning at the 110-minute mark).

Clinical facts in the question bank were checked against the CCI references above and the guideline sources
listed in `data/questions.json` `source` fields (e.g., AHA/ACCF/HRS recommendations for ECG standardization
2007–2009, AHA exercise-standards scientific statement 2013, AHA ACLS 2020 guidelines).
